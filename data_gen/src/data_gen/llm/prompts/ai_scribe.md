Kind of note: the note an ambient AI scribe drafts from a recorded visit conversation, for the clinician to review and sign.

It is a structured clinical note, not a transcript: it turns what was said aloud into plain, complete clinical sentences in the third person ("The patient reports...", "The patient denies..."), under headings. It uses full words, not abbreviations, and paraphrases the patient instead of quoting them. It records only what was discussed in the visit, so a lab value appears only as a result that was reviewed or discussed with the patient, never as a table. It is thorough and a little wordy rather than terse. An uncertain symptom stays uncertain ("is unsure whether", "occasionally"). The headings, the attribution verb, where the lab values go and the closing are set by the style lines. About 90 to 170 words.
