Setting: shorthand. A rushed outpatient note in clinician shorthand.

Write it as sentence fragments and common abbreviations (c/o, denies, f/u, RTC, pt, poss, +, -). "c/o" means "complains of": use it only for symptoms that are present or possible, and never write "c/o denies" or "c/o no"; a denied symptom is "denies X", "no X" or "- X". Keep it very short, about 15 to 50 words, in one or two short lines. Mention only the headline items listed. No paragraphs, no headings, no full sentences, no explanations. The order, lab format, grouping and closing fragment are set by the style lines.
