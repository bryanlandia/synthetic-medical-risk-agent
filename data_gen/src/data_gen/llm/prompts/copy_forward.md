Setting: copy_forward. A progress note built by copying the previous visit's note forward and editing it.

Write it as a progress note with routine boilerplate (for example "Patient seen in follow-up. Medications and history reviewed with patient." without naming any medication), some unchanged carried-over lines, and a few updated lines for this visit. Present the symptoms in a short list or compact lines, and the lab results in a short results line or list. Do not label any value as old, previous or carried over: every value looks current. About 90 to 160 words.
